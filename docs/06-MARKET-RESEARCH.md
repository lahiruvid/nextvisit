# Market research

Date: 14 October 2026

## How this was done

1. Name the buyer and the job they already pay for.
2. Check if big companies already own that job.
3. Check if new companies are selling the same idea, and at what price.
4. Separate the world market from what one person in Sri Lanka can sell.
5. Pick the more stable category if the first idea fails step 2 or step 4.

## ApproveTrail

The idea is real. Companies will want a saved record when software sends an email, a refund, or a data change.

Forecasts, not promises:

- Futurum, 2026: operations and workflow software toward about $92 billion by 2030.
- Forrester: AI governance software more than quadruples by 2030, to about $15.8 billion.
- Gartner, February 2026: AI governance platform spend can pass $1 billion by 2030.

People already selling this:

- Auditant, Arahi, Avowex, Pinksheep (from about $29 a month), AgentValet (about $99 to $699 a month).
- Airlock and Gabriel Operator are small teams in Europe doing the same kind of control.

Buyers are mostly US and European software teams. They buy from a vendor they can call. A new product from Matale is hard to sell to them.

## Claims software

Large market. Already owned. Guidewire claims revenue was about $980 million in fiscal year 2024, with 270+ insurer customers. Duck Creek and others sit beside it. Do not build a claims product.

## More stable category

Software tied to work that cannot stop: a visit, a repair, a cleaning job, a clinic appointment. The buyer must keep a record and send the next step. Reviews of business software in 2026 show these buyers cancel less often than marketing tools.

He already knows this buyer. Okitha Service is a cleaning company. ArkaTasks already does this job. A second generic AI-record product does not.

## What one person can sell

Not the billion-dollar forecasts.

A fair first target, if he talks to shops himself:

- Price about $20 to $50 a month.
- 10 paying shops is about $200 to $500 a month.
- 30 paying shops is about $600 to $1,500 a month.

That takes years. It can be $0 if nobody is asked to pay.

## Decision

Keep the two Java services. They are the skill foundation.

Do not sell ApproveTrail as the main product. The stable product is a job record for a service business: one job saved, one follow-up queued. Same engine. Clearer buyer.
